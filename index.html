Welcome to AuraBook Hub Platform

A comprehensive digital ecosystem for exchanging old books, purchasing new ones, donating to underprivileged students, and reading/downloading free e-books instantly.

The Inspiration 💡 Idea

Many students struggle with the high cost of new academic textbooks, while thousands of read books sit idle in homes. We created Book Hub to recycle knowledge and make books freely circular.

Our Mission 🎯 Purpose

To build a 100% accessible educational gateway where students can exchange used books seamlessly, buy low-cost second-hand books, or access our built-in PDF Reader for immediate study.

Key Features 🚀 Facilities

  • 1-to-1 Book Exchange System
  • Buy & Sell New/Used Textbooks
  • Book Donation for Needy Students
  • Integrated E-PDF Reader & Downloads

How to Buy Books?

Get your required books delivered directly to your village in 3 easy steps.

1
Search & Select

Browse new or used books available in your or nearby villages.

2
Place Order

Review details, select Cash on Delivery, and confirm your village address.

3
Village Delivery

Our delivery team hands over the book right at your doorstep.

How to Sell Used Books?

Earn cash by selling books you no longer need to other students.

1
Click “Sell Book”

Fill a quick 1-minute form with basic details about your book.

2
Upload Photos & Price

Take clear photos of cover pages and set your selling price.

3
Get Paid on Pickup

Once ordered, our agent collects the book from your home and pays you.
